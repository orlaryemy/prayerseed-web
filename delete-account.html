Delete Your Account

You're always in control of your data.

You can permanently delete your PrayerSeed account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how, in line with the PrayerSeed app (developer: Olayemi Michael Awoeyo).

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the PrayerSeed app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are removed immediately.

If you'd like a copy of your data first, use Settings → Export My Data before deleting.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the email address on your account at support@prayerseed.app with the subject "Delete my account." We will verify your request and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account and profile
  • Your children's profiles (names, dates of birth, photos)
  • All prayers, stories, notes and favourites you created

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Limited records may be retained only where required by law, and are otherwise deleted.

Note about subscriptions: If you have a PrayerSeed+ subscription, deleting your account does not cancel billing. Manage or cancel your subscription in your device's App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) account settings.

Questions?

Contact us any time at support@prayerseed.app.
