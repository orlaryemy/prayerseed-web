Frequently asked questions

Everything parents ask before downloading.

What is PrayerSeed?

PrayerSeed is a prayer app for Christian parents. It creates personalised morning, midday and night prayers and Bible-based bedtime stories for each of your children, using their name, age and what they're going through — so praying for your kids becomes a simple daily habit.

What ages is PrayerSeed for?

Parents use PrayerSeed for children from babies and toddlers through to teens. Every prayer and bedtime story is written for your child's age, so a prayer for a three-year-old reads very differently from one for a thirteen-year-old.

Is PrayerSeed free?

Yes. PrayerSeed is free to download and free accounts include daily prayers and 3 personalised bedtime stories a month. PrayerSeed+ unlocks unlimited prayers and stories, all Bible translations, family and milestone prayers and audio narration, with a 7-day free trial you can cancel any time.

Are the prayers and stories based on the Bible?

Yes. Every prayer and bedtime story is woven with Scripture and comes with a Bible verse. You can choose from multiple Bible translations and follow weekly virtue themes such as kindness, courage and gratitude.

Can I use it for more than one child?

Yes. Add a profile for each of your children and every prayer and story is created just for them. Co-parents can share a child's profile so the whole family prays together.

Is my family's information private?

Yes. We never sell your data, and you can export or permanently delete your account and your children's profiles from inside the app at any time. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
